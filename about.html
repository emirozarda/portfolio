I'm a Mechanical Engineer-in-Training based in Vancouver, BC, and a recent Mechanical Engineering graduate from UBC. I wasn't always set on design — even partway through my degree I wasn't sure exactly where I'd end up. That changed during an early co-op term, where I got my first real exposure to the design process and started working seriously with CAD and CAM software. Since then I've steered my coursework and work terms toward mechanical design specifically, and it's become the part of engineering I care most about.

Today I split my time between CAD-heavy design work — SolidWorks, Fusion 360, and Siemens NX — and the hands-on side of turning a model into a physical part: machine shop time, FEA validation, and producing production-ready drawings that a shop can actually build from. I like being close to both ends of that process, from a first concept sketch to watching a part come off a CNC mill or 3D printer.

Outside of work, I swim, cook, and make YouTube content about the NBA. If you want to talk about a game, a recipe, or a project, feel free to reach out — my contact details are on the Contact page.
