Overview

Smart Carrier was a self-driving robot project built around a drivetrain designed from scratch — selecting gear ratios and a gearmotor to hit specific torque and speed targets, rather than adapting an off-the-shelf drive system.

02

Design Process

  • MOTION

    Wheel & frame configuration study

    Ran a SolidWorks Motion Study across different wheel and frame configurations to compare their torque and RPM demands before committing to a drivetrain layout — that comparison drove a 75% reduction in torque and RPM requirements versus the baseline configuration.

  • DRIVETRAIN

    Gear ratio & gearmotor selection

    Designed the drivetrain from the ground up, selecting gear ratios and a gearmotor sized to meet the torque-speed targets identified in the motion study.

  • STRUCTURE

    Load-rated chassis

    Built a chassis from wood and aluminum, rated to withstand 500 N loads, balancing weight against the structural demands of carrying and moving payloads.
