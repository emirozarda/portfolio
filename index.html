SHEET 01 — FLAGSHIP PROJECT

The Quadcopter

A search-and-rescue drone frame, generatively designed, stress-validated, and manufactured two ways: 3D printed nylon and CNC-machined aluminum.

Full Case Study

It started with a simple problem: Turkey sits across several major fault lines, and post-disaster search-and-rescue teams need small, tough aircraft that can fly into collapsed structures. I designed a quadcopter frame around that mission in Autodesk Fusion 360, using generative design to strip mass out of the structure while keeping it strong enough to survive hard landings.

The result was validated with static stress analysis, then built two ways — a nylon 3D-printed version and a CNC-machined aluminum alternative cut on a waterjet — to compare how the same design holds up across manufacturing methods.
